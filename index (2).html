📱 M-Pesa Payment

Pay any county fee quickly using M-Pesa Paybill. Use your Application ID as the account number.

Paybill Number
222 333
  1. 1 Go to M-Pesa → Lipa na M-Pesa → Pay Bill
  2. 2 Business No: 222333
  3. 3 Account No: Your Application ID
  4. 4 Enter amount & confirm with PIN
  5. 5 You will receive an SMS confirmation
